Who it is for

NGOs and programmes (donor reports, translation, qualitative analysis) · Service SMEs (accounting, insurance, real estate, agencies) · Transport and delivery (scheduling, multilingual communication with drivers, compliance) · Schools and institutions (administration, communication with families in several languages).

How it works

  1. Scoping — 30 minutesTogether we set the boundary: one team, one department, one process.
  2. Observation — days 1 to 5Interviews with your teams, observation of the real work, measurement of volumes and turnaround times. No technical decision at this stage.
  3. Analysis — days 6 to 9Every task is sorted: delegate, supervise, or keep human. Gain, effort and risk are quantified.
  4. Findings workshop — 90 minutesWe present the map, the roadmap and the risks to your decision-makers. You choose what comes next, or you stop there — no commitment.

What is not included

Building the automations themselves (that is the Guided pilot), software licence purchases, and any promise of quantified results before the real work has been observed.
